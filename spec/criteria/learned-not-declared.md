## Why
The product's claim is that the result is personal. The moment the system holds an
opinion of its own about resolution, tilt, or subject matter, the result becomes
somebody else's taste wearing the user's photographs, and the comparisons the user
made stop being the thing that decided the outcome.

## Guidance
- Let a shortcoming be penalized only as much as the user's own decisions imply.
- Admit marginal photos and let them rank low, rather than excluding them in advance.
- Distinguish deciding order from deciding a cutoff; comparisons give the first and
  never the second.

## Avoid
- Any fixed rule that promotes or demotes a photo before the user has judged anything
  like it.
- Confusing content screening, which is categorical, with taste, which is not.

## Assessment
Ask of any ranking behavior: which of the user's decisions produced this, and what
would it do differently for a user whose choices ran the other way?
